Owner playtest 2026-10-05 (`feedback/playtest.md`, `feedback/notes.md`, local and read-only). Rules are recorded in `docs/SPEC.md` 2.6 as "OWNER decision 2026-10-05" (the [ORIGINAL] Bomb and Rocket rows are overridden, original text kept as history). ## 1. Diagnosis (read from code; every gift is host-simulated, `SpecialBehavior` child of the gift `Block`)

Cross-cutting causes:

- **Settle gate uses Jolt `sleeping`.** `PropellerEffect.physics_tick` (game/specials/PropellerEffect.gd:84-89), `JumpingBeanEffect.physics_tick` (JumpingBeanEffect.gd:167-173) and `AnvilEffect.wants_early_trigger` (AnvilEffect.gd:62-63) wait for `block.sleeping`. A body sleeps only when its whole island is under `sleep_velocity_threshold=0.03` (project.godot:410). In a real match the disc tilts, other blocks move and a gift sits on a stack, so the island often never sleeps (see the sleep-onset findings on Bontago-1pi.11.47, "rare 3 to >10 s"). Meanwhile `SpecialBehavior.advance` force-triggers every special at `arm_delay + fuse_timeout_s` = 6.4 s (SpecialBehavior.gd:124-125; all tres use 6.0, bean 20.0). Result: effect never starts, fuse ends it silently. This is the Propeller "works sometimes" and the Bean "doesn't jump / no holes" (a hop only runs after the first settle, and the hole then needs `HoleMode != OFF`, MatchTerritory.gd:982).
- **Explosion strength is not mass-aware.** `SpecialPhysics.explode` (SpecialPhysics.gd, `pow(1.0 - ratio, 2.0)` falloff, `clampf(impulse * falloff, 0, max_impulse)`) applies a kg*m/s impulse capped at `SpecialTuning.max_explosion_impulse = 30` (SpecialTuning.gd:42). A cube is `cube_mass = 2.0` (physics_tuning.tres:9) and a tetromino 8 kg, so delta-v is 18 / 8 * falloff, i.e. about 2 m/s at the centre and about 0.5 m/s at half radius against `block_friction = 0.85`: "does nothing".
- **Gift body is also a stack member.** Effects bind to the placed carrier `Block` (MatchPlacement._attach_pending_special:740-750) and `_on_gift_completed` removes it 0.75 s after trigger (SpecialTuning.gift_despawn_delay_s). A standalone world object (Volcano) has no home today; `BlackHoleEffect.detonate` + `BlackHoleField` (parented to `Match.blocks_parent()`) is the working precedent.

## 2. Shared effect modules

New folder `game/specials/fx/`, static-typed, scene-tree-light, no per-block state on shared `SpecialEffect` resources (keep the existing block-meta pattern or a small `RefCounted` handle). Every number lives in a tuning `Resource` under `config/specials/fx/` embedded in the gift's `.tres` (no magic numbers). Existing `SpecialPhysics` stays; the new modules are the composition layer.

| Module | Signature (typed) | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `ExplosionFx` | `static func blast(space: PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D, center: Vector3, t: ExplosionTuning, exclude: Array[RID]) -> Array[RigidBody3D]`: delta-v per body = `t.peak_speed_mps * falloff(ratio, t.falloff_exponent)` along (outward + `t.upward_bias` up), applied as `impulse = delta_v * body.mass` through `SpecialPhysics.wake_and_impulse`; clamp is `t.max_delta_v_mps`, not kg*m/s. Includes chain trigger helper `chain(behavior, center, radius, depth)`. | Bomb, Rocket, Volcano orb |
| `DiscForce` | `static func apply(field: Field, world_pos: Vector3, sign: float, t: DiscForceTuning, delta: float) -> void` (tilt impulse toward/away; sign +1 Anvil dips the landing side, -1 Propeller raises it) and `static func shake(field: Field, t: DiscForceTuning, elapsed: float, delta: float) -> void` | Anvil, Propeller, Earthquake |
| `HolePunch` | `static func punch(world_pos: Vector3, radius_m: float, open_s: float) -> bool` (converts via `Match.field().disk_local_from_world`, honours HoleMode.OFF and PLAYING via `Match.punch_special_hole`; returns whether a hole was requested) | Jumping Bean (per hop) |
| `RadialPull` | `static func pull(space: PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D, center: Vector3, t: RadialPullTuning, exclude: Array[RID], filter: Callable, delta: float, on_captured: Callable = Callable()) -> Array[RigidBody3D]`: mass-proportional acceleration (`accel_mps2 * falloff`) plus a tunable `friction_compensation` term, no force cap in kg*m/s; `on_captured(body)` fires when a body enters `t.core_radius_m` | Magnet (no capture), Black hole (capture) |
| `BlockSpawner` | `static func spawn(shape: BlockShape, world_origin: Vector3, basis: Basis, owner_slot: int, velocity: Vector3, cap: int) -> Block` wrapping `Match` placement's `spawn_special_projectile` path (host only, no stat inflation, active-body cap) | Volcano, Stackfall |
| `LandedProbe` | `RefCounted` per block: `func update(block: Block, delta: float) -> void`, `func has_landed() -> bool`. Landed = linear speed < `t.landed_speed_mps` for `t.landed_hold_s`, OR first contact plus `t.landed_timeout_s` ceiling. Replaces every `block.sleeping` gate. `landed_age()` returns seconds since landing. | Bean, Propeller, Anvil, Magnet (optional) |
| `GiftBlink` | visual-only, client-computed from `Block.gift_id` + local age since spawn (no new RPC): `static func apply(block: Block, period_s: float, duration_s: float)`; emissive flash on the gift visual. | Bomb (rocket optional) |
| `GiftFxPresenter` | Node owned by `Match`, subscribes once to `Events.special_triggered` and dispatches a def-id to builder table (Callable) so per-gift client visuals stop adding handlers to `autoload/Match.gd` (today `_on_paintball_triggered`, `_on_black_hole_triggered` at Match.gd:134-135, 226). | Bomb/Rocket flash, Volcano structure, Black hole, Paintball |

Tuning resources (new, in `config/specials/fx/`): `ExplosionTuning` (radius_m, peak_speed_mps, falloff_exponent, upward_bias, max_delta_v_mps), `DiscForceTuning` (strength, shake_amplitude_m, shake_tilt_deg, duration_s), `RadialPullTuning` (radius_m, accel_mps2, falloff_exponent, friction_compensation, core_radius_m), `LandedTuning` (landed_speed_mps, landed_hold_s, landed_timeout_s). `SpecialTuning` gains `fixed_throw_speed_mps`, `fixed_throw_loft_deg` and drops the drag-throw knobs only after the throw package lands.

Lifecycle hooks added to `SpecialEffect`/`SpecialBehavior` (package B): `needs_landing() -> bool`, `lifetime_s() -> float` (replaces the blanket fuse; fuse stays only as a per-def safety backstop and is derived from lifetime), `detaches() -> bool` (true: host removes the carrier at trigger without the 0.75 s linger and the effect owns a standalone world node, as `BlackHoleEffect` does), `triggers_on_impact() -> bool` replacing today's `impact_triggers` veto (Bomb false, Rocket true), `impact_filter` ignoring the owner's own thrower for the first `arm_delay`.

## 3. Per-gift behaviour and tunables (starting values, all in resources)

- **Bomb** (decided): dropped, lands, blinks `blink_duration_s = 3.0` (period 0.25 s, accelerating to 0.1), then explodes regardless of impacts; a chain trigger from a neighbouring blast still detonates it early. `ExplosionTuning`: radius 5.0 m, peak 12 m/s delta-v, falloff exponent 1.5, upward_bias 0.35, max 16 m/s. Carrier removed at explosion (existing despawn). Bomb is a throwable gift candidate (Q1).
- **Rocket** (decided pending Q1 launch direction): launched on the fixed trajectory (see throw), flies under thrust `thrust_speed_mps = 14`, `explode_on_impact = true` via contact with any Block/Field/map (continuous CD armed), safety `fuel_duration_s = 4.0` then explode. `ExplosionTuning` radius 4.5 m, peak 10 m/s.
- **Volcano** (decided): activation spawns `VolcanoStructure` (standalone `Node3D` under `Match.blocks_parent()`, host-simulated, mirrored on clients via `GiftFxPresenter`) at the activation point on the disc, rises over `rise_s = 2.0` to `height_m = 4.0`, base radius 2.5 m, then erupts for `lifetime_s = 22.0` (spec says at least 20 s). Eruption interval random in `[eruption_interval_min_s 0.4, max 1.2]`, 1-3 blocks per burst, cone 35 degrees, launch speed 9 m/s, owner-coloured cube blocks via `BlockSpawner` (not counted as placements, respects the 600 cap). Orb impact blast `ExplosionTuning` radius 1.5, peak 4 m/s as today's `VolcanoOrbEffect`. The gift carrier despawns at trigger. Riding the disc tilt: structure is an `AnimatableBody3D` child of the disc (Q4).
- **Earthquake / Anvil** (unchanged behaviour): moved onto `DiscForce.shake` / `DiscForce.apply(sign=+1)`; Anvil's landing detect moves to `LandedProbe` (today `block.sleeping`, Anvil mass 60 sleeps fast so it works; the probe makes it deterministic).
- **Propeller** (decided, "Anvil in reverse"): on landing (`LandedProbe`), `DiscForce.apply(sign=-1)` for `duration_s = 3.0`, raising the landing side; strength set so total tilt is comparable to Anvil at the same distance (Anvil 0.02 per m, so about 0.02 x distance integrated over the window). The carrier's `linear_velocity.y` write is removed; an optional visual-only rise `carrier_rise_m = 0.6` on the gift visual keeps "lifts upward" without touching physics. DECISION: this drops the literal body lift; the disc coupling is the effect.
- **Jumping Bean** (decided): lands (`LandedProbe`), then every `hop_interval_s = 1.2` hops (`hop_speed_mps = 6.5`, horizontal 3.5, random yaw, STABLE-freeze released via `wake_for_impulse`) and calls `HolePunch.punch(pre-hop position, hole_radius_m = 1.4, hole_open_s = 2.5)`; `lifetime_s = 14.0` after landing. The fuse backstop is `lifetime_s + 5`. Hops use `Block.kick` as today.
- **Magnet** (decided): `RadialPull` with `radius_m = 8`, `accel_mps2 = 18` plus `friction_compensation = 8.3` (= block_friction * g, so net pull is real), duration 3 s, enemy-only filter kept (spec row). It moves blocks, no capture, no deletion. Distinct from Black hole by: enemy-only, no core, brief.
- **Glue** (decided): bonds survive normal play. Replace the per-tick `accel * mass` stress with a break test on joint load (relative linear separation above `break_separation_m = 0.15` or relative speed above `break_speed_mps = 6.0`) and raise stiffness; `GlueDropTuning.break_force` default 40 becomes 600 and the test above is the acceptance (a glued pair on a tilting disc stays together; a bomb blast at 2 m breaks it). Charges stay at the owner's spec value 3 (glue.tres currently 5; align or confirm in the brief).
- **Black hole** (decided strength, capture behaviour Q2): `RadialPull` radius 7 m, `accel_mps2 = 26`, `friction_compensation = 8.3`, `core_radius_m = 0.8`, lifetime 6 s, no kg*m/s cap. Capture default per Q2 recommendation.
- **Stackfall**: `block_count` 12 to 28, `spawn_height_m` 18 to 30, `blocks_per_second` 6 to 10 (owner said "maybe"; tunables only), spawning through `BlockSpawner`.
- **Paintball, Freeze, Earthquake**: untouched behaviour; do not regress.
- **Cat** (decided): disabled. `SpecialDef.enabled_by_default` is currently unused by the roster (MatchGifts.gd:_ensure_special_drawer_installed:616-633 draws `load_all_specials()`); add a real `SpecialDef.load_selectable_specials()` honouring it and use it in MatchGifts, the lobby gift checklist and `Sandbox.gd` cycle list (Sandbox.gd:143). `cat.tres` gets `enabled_by_default = false`; code and resource stay.
- **No rotation, no recolour** (decided): while the held piece is a gift, `PlayerController` ignores `rotate_*` input (PlayerController.gd:670-684) and the ghost/gift visual is not tinted by owner colour (GhostPreview validity tint still applies as a placement-validity cue, see risk). The spawned carrier uses identity yaw (host also forces identity basis for gifts so a hostile client cannot rotate it).
- **Throw only for some gifts, fixed trajectory** (default set Bomb, Rocket, Paintball, Q1): `SpecialDef.throwable: bool`; `PlayerController._can_begin_throw_aim` (PlayerController.gd:999-1009) returns false unless the held gift is throwable; `request_throw` (MatchPlacement.gd:345) keeps its RPC arguments but the host discards drag magnitude: velocity = aim yaw direction x `SpecialTuning.fixed_throw_speed_mps` (14) with `fixed_throw_loft_deg` (35). Pure maths in `core/gifts/GiftThrow.gd`.

## 7. Decisions recorded (minor, picked here)

- Settle detection is a velocity/timeout probe, not Jolt `sleeping`.
- Explosion/pull strength expressed as delta-v and acceleration so it is independent of block mass.
- Bomb blink visual is client-derived from `gift_id` and spawn age (no new RPC); explosion visual rides the existing `special_triggered`.
- Throw RPC unchanged; host normalises to the fixed trajectory (no `net/MatchNet.gd` change).
- Propeller drops the physical carrier lift (replaced by a visual-only rise); the disc tilt is the effect.
- Cat disabled through a real `enabled_by_default` filter, not by deleting content.
- Stackfall numbers raised as tunables only ("maybe" in the owner text).

4. **Is the Volcano a solid obstacle, and what happens to blocks in its footprint?** Options: (a) solid cone riding the disc; blocks under the footprint are pushed outward as it rises (proposed); (b) non-solid visual mountain, blocks pass through; (c) solid, and it refuses activation when the footprint overlaps blocks (the gift is then burned/kept queued).

## 9. Risks

- `LandedProbe` timeout ceiling can read "landed" while the gift is still sliding on a steep tilt; mitigated by the speed test being primary.
- Raising Glue stiffness on a 6-DOF joint can add solver cost with many bonds; keep the per-drop charge cap and bench the existing `bench_specials_chain`.
- `GiftBlink` runs on clients from local age, so blink phase can differ by the spawn-event latency; only the explosion moment is authoritative.
- Ghost recolour: validity tint (valid/invalid/hole) is a placement cue; I treated the owner's "no recolour" as removing owner-colour tint only. Confirm in the demo.
- Volcano structure collider on the tilting disc adds a moving kinematic body; if Q4 chooses (a), pushing blocks in the footprint needs a bounded scan to stay cheap.
- Package I touches `PlayerController.gd` (about 1700 lines, hotspot): keep its diff to the rotate gate, throw gate and tint, and run `test_playercontroller_gift_slot` plus throw tests.
