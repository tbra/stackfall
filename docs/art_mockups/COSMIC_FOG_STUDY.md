# Cosmic horror: fog and megalophobia study

These two 1672 × 941 RGB concept images explore the owner's new references under `feedback/references/cosmic horror` (five local JPGs inspected on 2026-10-03). The references are owner-local feedback and are **not** copied into this package.

- `cosmic_fog_colossus_tendrils.png`: several limbs and a remote central mass. The arena's small blocks and beacons establish scale.
- `cosmic_fog_colossus_underside.png`: less anatomy, with an enormous partial underside passing above the arena. This is the stronger test of an uncertain, frame-breaking creature.

Both are concept art, not a ready panorama, sky texture, map scene, or approved design. They intentionally keep the arena readable and the organism outside the full frame. The earlier crowned silhouette in `Bontago-mp0.57` remains on hold after the owner said that direction missed the target. A later art pass should confirm creature visibility and fog density with the owner before building the environment asset.

Beyond it, layers of cool blue-gray fog obscure an immense soft-edged mass and several long organic limbs or tendrils that disappear above and below the frame. Do not reveal a complete creature. Keep the horizon ambiguous and the creature much larger than the arena, quiet and unsettling rather than aggressive. Diffuse overcast light, low contrast in the distance, near-black arena rim for foreground separation. Preserve the atmospheric scale and obscured silhouettes seen in the references without copying a particular composition.
