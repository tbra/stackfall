Block seam style Q2

(a) Cell-grid emissive seams

Thin emissive lines on the merged mesh's cell boundaries; the shape still reads as one solid block. Literal §2.10 reading.

(b) Silhouette outline only

One glowing edge around the whole block. Simpler; loses interior structure.

(c) Rim-light shader term

Fresnel glow, no seam geometry. Cheapest, most stylised.
