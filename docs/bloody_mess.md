In my preview right now player 2 places a block, then player 3 and then player 4. disregarding the fact that player 1 doesn't work(they're just skipped) that's not how the game is supposed to work. Everyone plays at the same time, each player gets a block(not shared, not the same) that they have to place before their timer runs out. When they place their block they immediately get a new block and a new timer starts, no waiting for other players. they can only place the block within their area of influence, at the start that's just a small circle around their start flag(+some extra space). when you place a block(again, has to be within your existing area) a new circle expands from that block, at first it's very small but if you stack blocks on top the circle grows based on the height of the stacked blocks. 
The goal is to capture the goal flags on the map. you accomplish this by making your area of influence encapsulate the base of the flag. to make it more challenging the goal flags have their own area of influence and within that area no blocks are allowed to be placed by the players(that's the "hole" mechanic you've been reading about). In order to capture the flag(s) and win you'll need to build 1 or more connecting towers so that your area of influence creates an undisrupted path between you start flag and all goal flags.
So, to reiterate, stack blocks to create a circular area of influence which scales in size proportionally with the height of the stack. You could either build a very tall tower to win or multiple smaller ones. you can only drop blocks within your existing area of influence. if the stack falls for some reason, due to physics or powerups or whatever, the circle should update to reflect that so it should constantly be updating the area, not just when a new block is placed.
Don't overcomplicate things when trying to solve the above. just do a raycast from the middle of the ghost block and straight down to determine whether the block can be placed or not(has to be inside your own area of influence and not within the goal flags area of influence). Right now you're using some kind of cell/grid system which produces a jagged area of influence, I think some kind off metaballs system would work better so the area is more smooth.
Different players areas of infleunce can't overlap so when players get close to eachother the height of the tower should "push" on the border, it's not a binary p1 has a higher stack so they get the whole area but p1 should still get more of the shared area if their stack is taller(again, measured from the individual stacks that produces the contested area and not a global value)
