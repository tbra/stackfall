# Beacon-claim tension audio (placeholder, Bontago-1pi.114 package C)

Three original procedural cues, generated by `tools/generate_claim_tension.py` (stdlib only, fixed seed, byte-identical reruns). All are mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM, normalised to the project convention (-18 dBFS 0.3 s window RMS, -1 dBFS peak cap).

| File | Length | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/effects/claim_tension_loop.wav` | 8.00 s, seamless loop | Low drone, 1 Hz double heartbeat, shimmer swelling mid-loop. One loop serves every level: runtime scales volume and pitch. Rival captures reuse it quieter/darker. |
| `assets/effects/claim_interrupt_sting.wav` | 1.00 s | Descending detuned stab plus low thud, hard cut: your claim just broke. |
| `assets/effects/claim_rival_broken.wav` | 0.60 s | Bright rising three-note cue: you broke a rival's hold. |

Loop seam: every drone/shimmer frequency completes whole cycles in 8 s, the pulse period (1 s) divides 8 s and the shimmer envelope is zero at both ends, so last-to-first sample step is within normal sample-to-sample motion.

Audition via `docs/audio_review/sfx_audition_reel.wav` (last three clips). Headless checks cannot judge sound: listen for a pleasant (not annoying) 8 s repeat, clear heartbeat, and a sting distinct from the rival-broken cue. Commissioned or Codex-made loops can later replace these by filename.
