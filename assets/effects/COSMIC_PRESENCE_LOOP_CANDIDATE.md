# Cosmic presence ambience candidate

`cosmic_presence_loop_candidate.wav` is an original 12-second dry mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM ambience loop. It uses three quiet low harmonic tones, a distant pulse, and filtered circular air noise. All components repeat mathematically after 12 seconds, so a loop player can wrap without a deliberate fade or a long silent tail. The full source is `tools/generate_cosmic_presence_loop.py`; no samples or licensed material were used.

Intended use is a subtle bed beneath the deferred cosmic horror map's fog and immense moving creature. It is a candidate, not a replacement for music or current effects. Audition it at low gameplay volume with placement, gift, and voice cues before any scene or mixer wiring. Adjust gain and EQ in context; the digital seam and peak checks do not establish that it sounds right by ear.
