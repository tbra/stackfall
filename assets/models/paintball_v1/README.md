# Faceted paintball gift candidate

`paintball_v1.glb` is an original small held-gift art candidate. A faceted blue ball carries a broad coral front splash, brass and mint spots, and three large splash tips that break the round silhouette. The dark capsule seam preserves the toy-like read at small scale. It is about 0.9 units wide and 0.8 units high, pivoted at the ball center. The GLB contains model meshes only.

`source_art/paintball_v1/paintball_v1.blend` is editable Blender source under `.gdignore`. `paintball_v1_preview.png` is the neutral 960 × 720 render. Rebuild with Blender 4.5: `blender -b --python tools/generate_paintball_model.py`.

This is an art candidate. `assets/gifts/held/paintball.tscn` remains active; no scene or gameplay logic is changed.
