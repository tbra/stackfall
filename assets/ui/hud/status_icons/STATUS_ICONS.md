# HUD status pictogram candidates

Five standalone 32×32 SVG source icons: timer, height, territory, gift, and locked. They map to readouts and states in `ui/HUD.gd` and `ui/HUD.tscn`, but are not wired into the game. The labels and numbers should remain visible when icons are used; the art is supplemental.

`docs/art_mockups/hud_status_icons_v1.svg` and its PNG render show 48, 32, and 24 px sizes. Sources are generated by a script removed in Bontago-dbn.1 (see git history). Keep these dark-ink pictograms on paper or field surfaces. Review against bright sky and dark storm with the intended HUD panel behind them before integration.
